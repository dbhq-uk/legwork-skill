# Answer key: UK banks with a public API sandbox, and what each requires before you can call it

How the list was built: candidates came from two registers, not memory - the Open Banking Tracker dataset (`not-a-bank/open-banking-tracker-data`, 125 UK-linked providers with a developer portal or sandbox flag) and Open Banking Ltd's own list of 76 enrolled Account Providers (openbanking.org.uk `wp-json/wp/v2/directory`, provider type "Account Providers"), topped up with Google (Bright Data, gb) and WebSearch sweeps for "<bank> developer portal / open banking sandbox / third party providers" across the CMA9, challengers, building societies, specialist and business banks, and UK arms of foreign banks.
Every row was then checked on the bank's own developer portal or documentation (or the vendor-hosted portal the bank's own site links to) on 27 Sep 2026; each quote was grep-checked against a saved copy of the page in `/tmp/keys-banks/`.

**Scope rule used for the two tables.** Table 1 holds UK-authorised banks and building societies whose sandbox is publicly documented and whose own pages do not make FCA/NCA authorisation a precondition for sandbox access. Open Banking Directory (sandbox) enrolment, certificates, app registration or an email request are recorded as the requirement. Table 2 holds banks checked and found with no public sandbox, a dead or offline one, a sandbox whose own page limits it to regulated TPPs, or an institution that is not a bank.

**Scoring note on "Open Banking Directory enrolment".** Several Table 1 rows need an SSA or certificates from the Open Banking Directory (sandbox). OBL says of enrolment: "Part of these checks include making sure your company has the right permissions under PSD2 i.e., that you are an ASPSP, AISP and/or PISP" ([openbanking.org.uk/directory](https://www.openbanking.org.uk/directory/)); the same page says technical service providers can also use the Directory Sandbox. A report that says these sandboxes are in practice open only to regulated TPPs (or their technical providers) is correct; a report that says they need only a free developer account is wrong.

**Three types of requirement, from lightest to heaviest:**

- **Self-serve: a free account, then an app or key.** Starling, Revolut, Chase UK, Griffin, Handelsbanken, Cynergy, Kroo, ABN AMRO and J.P. Morgan Private Bank. HSBC and Barclays add their own checks or test certificates. NatWest is self-serve too, and its sandbox pages list no certificate or Directory step.
- **Open Banking Directory (sandbox) SSA and certificates, with DCR:** Lloyds, Santander, Nationwide, Virgin Money, TSB, Monzo (OB API), Tesco, Zopa, Allica, Zempler, Vanquis, Investec, UBL, Zenith and C. Hoare.
- **eIDAS test certificates, or either kind:** Danske and Bank of Ireland take test eIDAS certificates as well as Directory ones. Deutsche Bank and Triodos (self-generated) use test eIDAS or Berlin Group certificates, and Habib, Bank of East Asia and Nomo need Salt Edge OBSeal test certificates.

**Watch-outs for scoring Table 1**
