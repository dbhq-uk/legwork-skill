# Answer key: UK flat-pack campervan furniture kits for a Ford Transit L3H2, and what a complete kit costs

How the list was found: about 60 Google (GB) searches through Bright Data plus WebSearch, covering flat pack, self-build, DIY kit, pod, furniture pack, conversion kit, Transit Mk8 / L3 / L3H2 / LWB medium roof / 350 / Jumbo, eBay, Etsy, Gumtree, Reddit and Facebook groups (Ford Transit Camper, Van Conversions Into Campers, vanlife.uk), then every named supplier followed to its own site.
Every entry below was checked on the supplier's own page (fetch.py, Bright Data scrape/render, or WebFetch) on 27 Sep 2026. Main finding for scoring: no UK supplier found sells a complete flat-pack kit labelled for the Transit L3H2 (medium roof); the one named complete Transit kit (EVO Design 10) is labelled L3H3 (high roof). A report that says otherwise is wrong. Many "Transit" kits are for the smaller Transit Custom, which is a different van.
